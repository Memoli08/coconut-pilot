# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Coconut Pilot to be a welcoming place for users and contributors of
every background, experience level and platform preference.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and specific.
- Focus criticism on code, behavior and ideas, never a person.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions before escalating a conflict.
- Respect differing accessibility, language and technical-experience needs.
- Avoid sharing another person's private information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Deliberate disruption, intimidation or repeated bad-faith communication.
- Publishing private information, credentials or security-sensitive details.

## Reporting

For behavior that cannot safely be discussed in public, contact the repository
owner privately through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do
not open a public issue with sensitive details. Security vulnerabilities follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that violate
these standards.
